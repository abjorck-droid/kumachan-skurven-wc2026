   - Deployed to **Cloudflare Pages** (project `kumachan-skurven-wc2026`, connected to the repo,
     build output dir `site`, build command `exit 0`, env vars `AIRTABLE_PAT` + `AIRTABLE_BASE_ID`).
     **Live at `https://kumachan-skurven-wc2026.pages.dev`.** Confirmed end-to-end: token auth,
     a real pick landed in `Predictions`.

5. **Group-match picks.** Built `scripts/load_fixtures.py` (one-time load of the schedule into
   `Matches`) — **72 group fixtures loaded** (knockouts unpublished). Added `match_outcome`
   predictions (Home/Draw/Away + optional exact score, per-match token + pundit note) to the local
   server, the Function, and the UI. Confirmed match picks save to `Predictions`.

6. **Champion bonus locked at 150** (was 100). Reasoning: keeps the champion clearly the biggest
   "normal" bracket call (~1.9 full tiers) while an All-In champion (5×150 = 750) stays *under* the
   Dark Horse's signature 1000 ceiling, preserving the risk/reward hierarchy. 200 was the considered
   cap (would tie the All-In Dark Horse) and rejected. Spec bumped to **v1.0 (locked)** and renamed
   `03_Scoring_spec_v1.0.md`; also corrected the lock-semantics table (knockout per-match picks
   can't lock at tournament kickoff — pairings don't exist yet).

7. **Scoring engine.** Built `scripts/scoring_engine.py` — recomputes `points_awarded` +
   `beat_rival_bonus` per prediction and rolls up `PoolPlayers.total_score`. Covers: match outcome
   (+exact, round-tiered), bracket slots (resolved from a teams-reached-each-round map), Dark Horse
   ladder (live until the Final), side games (compared to `SideGames.resolved_value`, incl.
   total-goals "closest without going over" across both players), Beat-Cal +3, confidence-token
   multipliers (soft downside), mulligan 50%. Pure functions unit-tested; full pipeline
   integration-tested with synthetic data. **Wired into `scoreboard-poll` as a second step**, so
   scores recompute after every poll.

8. **Tabbed pick-entry refactor.** Split the long single page into four tabs —
   **Group matches · Knockouts · Side bets · Review & Lock** — with the token reserves and Save
   button pinned in the sticky header across all tabs (one budget spans everything), and a per-tab
   pick count. Dark Horse lives under **Knockouts**.
