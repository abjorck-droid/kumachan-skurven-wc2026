The one-point championship that wasn't

Had Cal answered Andreas's 36 solo matches at his own live average, he'd have added ≈653 points — but unvoiding hands Andreas his 610 actual points back: net ≈43 to Cal. The sting: Andreas's voided bets were his best material of the tournament.

Run the full no-abstention world — both players answer everything — and the main margin collapses from 179 to ≈58. The pot stood at N = 88. The conversion formula does the rest:

max(1, 58 − 88) = 1
The archive would record the 2026 World Cup as decided by a single point

Doc 08 called the abstention line "degenerate." It was right. The gate didn't just void bets — it voided the greatest finish this pool never had. It dies in the Euros 2028 ruleset.

Ruling — The Stoppage pot is a permanent rule from day one of Euros 2028. The main board was functionally decided on July 7; under v1.0 the Final would have been a formality.
